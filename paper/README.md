# Manuscript

## Role of this directory

The manuscript is the mathematical object of record for this project. Definitions, statements, and proofs are to be read from the manuscript. Everything else in this repository, including the validation material, is supporting material and does not substitute for it.

## Contents

- `manuscript_draft.docx`: the current draft manuscript. Its title in the draft is "The Extremal Moment Gap Between the Annihilation Number and the First Zagreb Index." The draft is dated September 2026 and is labelled a draft research manuscript.
- `supporting/proof_dossier.docx`: an internal working document that lays out the proof as a sequence of lemmas together with an audit checklist and audit history. Its status labels record the state of the audit at the time it was written. Where the dossier and the manuscript differ, the manuscript governs.

## Status

The manuscript is being prepared for submission to the *Electronic Journal of Combinatorics*. It has not been submitted, accepted, or published, and it has not been peer reviewed.

## Planned additions

A PDF (or LaTeX source) of the manuscript will replace or accompany the Word draft when available.
